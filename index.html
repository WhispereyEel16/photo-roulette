Rules & photography tips

1. Start from any public path in Auckland Domain. Good starting areas: Duck Ponds, Museum grounds or the paths near Wintergardens. Follow the route cards safely. Once you reach the end, find a subject within about 8 metres.

2. Spend up to three minutes finding a subject. Move a little to adjust your framing, but don't go searching for a new location.

3. Try to satisfy the subject, framing and lighting restrictions. If a mission becomes impossible, reroll the photo prompts but keep your walk. Each person can create a different image from the same challenge.

4. Paths first: never walk into roads, water, garden beds or restricted areas. Stop walking before checking the screen. Don't photograph strangers up close without permission. Wintergardens and managed areas have opening hours.

Low light? Find a brighter edge, steady your camera and raise ISO as needed. Never enter dark or unsafe areas just to satisfy the prompt. Adjust Lighting above and regenerate the photo restrictions.
